NEED TO KNOW

Good questions.
Clear answers.

See the essentials before your first purchase.

Do I need my Blood Strike password?+

No. This checkout uses the game's numeric Player ID. Never send your password or login codes to a reseller.

Can I send Gold to another account?+

Enter the intended recipient's numeric Player ID. Confirm it carefully before submitting, because fulfillment to the wrong account may not be reversible.

How long does delivery take?+

Timing depends on the selected payment and authorized supplier. Check your order page for the actual delivery state. The demo mode does not send real Gold.

Does the current website send real Gold?+

Only after a verified live payment service and authorized fulfillment account have been configured and enabled by the operator. A clearly labelled demo mode is provided for safe testing.
